The Influence of Corporate Social Responsibility on Firm Financial Performance in the Nigerian Insurance Industry

This page provides the abstract and access details for the paper.

Abstract

Investigating the impact of Corporate Social Responsibility on financial performance in Nigerian insurance firms. The study finds no significant short-term effect but advocates for a long-term strategy to build brand loyalty and value.
